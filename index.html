ROOFRUSH

Rip it. Roll it. Nail it. Beat the storm!

A free game by Cloudy · Reset progress

How to Play

  1. Tear off. Swipe or tap the old shingles to rip them off. Tough ones take more hits.
  2. Underlayment. Swipe across the bare deck to roll out the waterproof felt.
  3. Shingle it. A shingle row slides side to side. Tap when it lines up with the dashed chalk line. Closer = better.

Finish before the storm arrives! Stars come from speed and accuracy. Spend your earnings in the Garage on a better tool, nail gun, crew helper, and weather radio.
